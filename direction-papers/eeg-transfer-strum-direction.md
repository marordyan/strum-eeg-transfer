# EEG Transfer on STRUM, a Direction: the field's transfer claims are unfalsifiable as reported, and the corrective is reporting geometry rather than a larger model or a richer sensor set

A focused review across the four strands of the STRUM foundation-model transfer project. The corpus of 84 entries does not show that pretrained electroencephalography models fail to transfer; it shows that most published transfer claims cannot be checked, because a single reported number is one position on three axes the corpus has itself measured to be non-monotone and model-reordering: the unit that was held out, the adaptation regime, and the identity of the checkpoint. This paper argues that the project's contribution is therefore not a new checkpoint, a denser montage or a wider sensor inventory, but a change in what a transfer study reports: the axis rather than the point, with each position stated. STRUM earns its place in that programme because its factorial design, its published blockwise protocol and its co-recorded pair structure make three of those axes cheap to traverse on one recording ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)); it does not earn it as a sensor inventory, which is why the one planned comparison that treats it as a sensor inventory is the one the corpus says would not establish what it appears to. The argument was first articulated in the project's three planned comparisons and developed here against the seven surviving gaps and seven actionable routes of [`gap-analysis`](../research/synthesis/gap-analysis.md), the cross-strand themes of [`science-map`](../research/synthesis/science-map.md), and the four strand ontologies.

Abbreviations: EEG, electroencephalography; ECG, electrocardiography; EOG, electrooculography; EMG, electromyography; EDA, electrodermal activity; PPG, photoplethysmography; HRV, heart-rate variability; MEG, magnetoencephalography; CSP, common spatial patterns; LEACE, least-squares concept erasure; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; Az, area under the curve as reported by the STRUM authors; BCI, brain-computer interface; BIDS, Brain Imaging Data Structure; LSL, Lab Streaming Layer; XDF, Extensible Data Format; HED, Hierarchical Event Descriptors; STRUM, Small Team Reconnaissance Urban Missions; TUAB, Temple University Hospital Abnormal corpus; TUEV, Temple University Hospital Event corpus; CV, cross-validation; LOSO, leave-one-subject-out.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The arc

The checkpoint layer is mature enough to be inventoried rather than surveyed. Every carded checkpoint binds a channel to a place by exactly one of five mutually exclusive mechanisms: a learned per-channel parameter ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md), [biot-2023](../research/collection/eeg-models/biot-2023/card.md), [bendr-2021](../research/collection/eeg-models/bendr-2021/card.md), [femba-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/femba-2025/card.md)), a physical coordinate ([reve-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/reve-2025/card.md), [brainomni-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainomni-2025/card.md), [luna-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/luna-2025/card.md)), local context ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)), a learned remap onto a fixed superset ([eegpt-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/eegpt-2024/card.md)), or nothing at all ([brainwave](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainwave/card.md)). Because the taxonomy is exhaustive over the carded set, whether a given recording can be ingested by a given checkpoint is answerable in advance rather than by experiment, and that is the one question about these models the corpus can settle cleanly.

An evaluation layer arrived alongside the checkpoints and disagreed with itself about the most basic parameter. Five suites place the holdout boundary in five different places: within-session five-fold with no subject holdout ([moabb](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/moabb/card.md)), subject-level 8:1:1 over each subject's trials ([omnieeg-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/omnieeg-bench/card.md)), leave-subjects-out with group-wise CV ([brain4fms](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/brain4fms/card.md)), subject-disjoint at a ratio never stated ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)), and one of four per-task strategies including a plain random split ([neuralbench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/neuralbench/card.md)). The suites also disagree about whether the adaptation recipe is a nuisance parameter or part of the model, and no entry in the corpus converts between the five positions ([datasets-benchmarks-ontology](../research/synthesis/datasets-benchmarks-ontology.md) §2.2, [moabb](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/moabb/card.md)).

A critique layer then measured what those choices cost, and the measurements are larger than the effects the field reports. Moving from segment holdout to subject holdout costs 46.8 points and lands the result on an interval containing chance ([brookshire-2024-data-leakage](../research/collection/eeg-models/brookshire-2024-data-leakage/card.md)); replicating three published models across five datasets under participant-disjoint partitioning raises error on all five, with the schizophrenia set reaching 0.50 error against 0.50 chance ([kamrud-2021-data-partitioning](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/kamrud-2021-data-partitioning/card.md)); moving from 10-fold to LOSO costs 7.74 and 13.1 points on one four-modality input held otherwise fixed ([angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load/card.md)); and a session change alone bounds a loss at up to 12.7 points, a figure its card marks as an upper bound because it mixes a best-case with an average ([zheng-2018-emotionmeter](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/zheng-2018-emotionmeter/card.md)). The same layer supplies the null that the theoretical chance level is not ([combrisson-2015-chance-level](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/combrisson-2015-chance-level/card.md)) and the interval that a fold-wise standard error underestimates ([varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure/card.md)).

The fusion layer developed in parallel and asks a different question with a different vocabulary. Twenty-five entries in the peripheral strand partition exactly on which ablation arms exist, and only four supply three arms on one split ([multimodal-biosignals-ontology](../research/synthesis/multimodal-biosignals-ontology.md) §1). Two peripheral foundation models are released ([papagei-2024](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/papagei-2024/card.md), [mckeen-2025-ecg-fm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mckeen-2025-ecg-fm/card.md)), the engineered alternative is a dozen recurring HRV indices computed by a standard library ([haque-hrv-stress-review](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/haque-hrv-stress-review/card.md), [makowski-2021-neurokit2](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/makowski-2021-neurokit2/card.md)), and the dyadic setting the project targets is served by four multi-person EEG recordings of 2, 3, 4 and 2 simultaneous participants ([candidate-datasets-ontology](../research/synthesis/candidate-datasets-ontology.md) §6.2, [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

### 1.2 Gap statement

Eleven absences were named in Phase 4 and seven survive; the framing here is theirs and is not restated in new words. What is missing, in the corpus's own enumeration, is a comparison of post-hoc linear erasure against cross-validated confound regression on one dataset and one set of folds ([lin-2026-identity-trap](../research/collection/eeg-models/lin-2026-identity-trap/card.md), [snoek-2019-confound-control](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/snoek-2019-confound-control/card.md)); any measurement of what ocular-artifact removal costs in task accuracy ([mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md), [rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias/card.md)); any decoding result reported from EOG electrodes and from a simultaneous eye tracker on the same trials ([mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md)); a frozen EEG encoder paired with a frozen peripheral encoder ([papagei-2024](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/papagei-2024/card.md), [ding-2025-cross-attention-fusion](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/ding-2025-cross-attention-fusion/card.md)); a within-subject EEG decode of spoken versus written presentation ([simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories/card.md), [mous-2019](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/mous-2019/card.md)); a measured cross-participant synchronization precision in any multi-person recording ([boa-actors-2025](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/boa-actors-2025/card.md), [livewire-2024](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/livewire-2024/card.md)); and an open, dense, other-group dyadic EEG release, the open ones found being two small recordings from one laboratory ([livewire-2024](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/livewire-2024/card.md), [boa-actors-2025](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/boa-actors-2025/card.md)).

Those seven are absences of experiments. The absence this paper builds on is one level up and is visible only when they are held together: no entry in the corpus reports a transfer result as a position on the axes the corpus has demonstrated reorder results, and the two suites that come closest disagree in sign about the reordering they measure ([omnieeg-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/omnieeg-bench/card.md), [adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)). That is not itself a Phase 4 gap and is not offered as one; it is the reading of the seven, and the scope check that would license it as a property of the field has not been run ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §6, [openneuro-2021](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/openneuro-2021/card.md)).

### 1.3 Thesis

This paper argues that the project should treat transfer as a quantity with coordinates rather than a quantity with a value, and should spend STRUM's unusual properties on locating results along those coordinates rather than on maximising any one of them. Concretely: report the pair-disjoint and the subject-disjoint arm and state the difference; report the frozen, partially fine-tuned and fully fine-tuned regime rather than one of them; run at least two checkpoints whose spatial mechanisms differ, so that a null is attributable to transfer rather than to one channel contract; and report comparison 1 as a label-count curve rather than at a single operating point ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md), [labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)). Section 2 inventories the seven cross-strand themes the argument draws on. Section 3 develops the argument in seven beats. Section 4 is the anchor case, STRUM's own published model comparison, which demonstrates that the geometry is traversable on this recording and simultaneously measures its resolution floor ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). Section 5 states what the corpus does not support, as its own treatment. Section 6 discusses the reliability of this review, because every claim here rests on a card and 57 of 84 cards were wrong about something before an audit. Section 7 is the roadmap, Section 8 the coordination with adjacent efforts, Section 9 the discussion including the strongest objection, and Section 10 the conclusion.

## 2. Background, the seven themes

The Phase 1 corpus organizes 84 entries into four strands; the Phase 3 [`science-map`](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) inventories the themes that cross at least two of them, a theme qualifying only if it draws on cards from more than one strand. This section summarizes the seven the argument draws on, each at the width its cards support, and it is a summary of prior work rather than of the corpus's properties, which are Phase 4's subject ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md) is the target the collection was written against and holds no role in any model's or suite's pipeline).

**Theme 1, the holdout unit decides what a number means.** The corpus holds this property at four levels, and they are not interchangeable: as a property of the suite, of the checkpoint's own paper, of the fusion gain, and of the experimental design upstream of any protocol ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §1). The design level is the one the other three have no representation for: [mous-2019](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/mous-2019/card.md) manipulates spoken against written between 102 readers and 102 listeners, so no choice of holdout unit separates the label from subject identity. Identifier-level traps defeat a correctly chosen protocol at the file level, one participant appearing as three IDs across repeats ([tes-eeg-ecg-2021](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/tes-eeg-ecg-2021/card.md)) and 197 files over two nights per subject making a recording-level split something other than a subject-level split ([sleep-edfx](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/sleep-edfx/card.md)).

**Theme 2, models can score by recognizing identity.** The same failure mode appears at six levels of granularity, in four strands, in four vocabularies, with almost no citations between them ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §2). Dataset membership is decodable from frozen embeddings at AUROC 1.000 while the three-way diagnosis on the same pipeline reaches 0.528 ([zare-2026-stress-testing](../research/collection/eeg-models/zare-2026-stress-testing/card.md)); frozen embeddings of three checkpoints are dominated by subject identity at 13 to 89 times a random-Gaussian null in 12 of 12 model-by-dataset pairs ([lin-2026-identity-trap](../research/collection/eeg-models/lin-2026-identity-trap/card.md)); a CSP pipeline retrained to predict which trial a segment came from reaches median 99.6 to 100 percent ([rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias/card.md)); and a decoder trained on some exemplars and tested on an unseen concept separates exemplars rather than semantic classes ([simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories/card.md)). Five remedies are enumerated across three strands, four of them runnable on data and the fifth a research programme ([ritchie-2019-decoding-limits](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/ritchie-2019-decoding-limits/card.md)).

**Theme 3, spatial identity is an admissibility question with a known answer per checkpoint.** The models strand's five-mechanism split and the candidate strand's recording specifications are two halves of one contract, and the formats strand holds the third ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §3). The consequence that matters for this project is a compounding: name-indexed checkpoints cannot take 206 channels without selection or interpolation, and coordinate-driven checkpoints can take an arbitrary layout but need a coordinate per channel, which is exactly the field STRUM lacks ([dataset-hierarchy](../research/synthesis/dataset-hierarchy.md) §7, [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). One checkpoint needs neither, generating position from local context ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)).

**Theme 4, three temporal windows that do not meet.** A stimulus-locked EEG epoch is 2 to 4 seconds; the peripheral branch's integration window across the corpus is 5 to 20 seconds, the upper bound having been corrected downward from 60 when one card's segment length was found to belong to a cited comparator rather than to the paper itself ([papagei-2024](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/papagei-2024/card.md), [multimodal-biosignals-ontology](../research/synthesis/multimodal-biosignals-ontology.md) §8.3). The one entry that sweeps the window buys about 0.012 AUROC going from 2 s to 20 s, while its input dimensionality grows from 27 features to 464, so part of the gain is capacity rather than a longer view ([wang-2025-sedation-non-eeg](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/wang-2025-sedation-non-eeg/card.md)).

**Theme 5, whether peripheral physiology adds information.** Four strands hold four pieces of this question in four vocabularies ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §5). The readable gains run from +0.002 through a null to +12.6, and the two double-digit entries are the two whose denominators their own cards flag as unverifiable ([salam-eeg-ecg-stress](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/salam-eeg-ecg-stress/card.md), [kumar-2026-attention-eeg-ecg-stress](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/kumar-2026-attention-eeg-ecg-stress/card.md)). The benchmark strand's position is that the channels are present, standardized and discarded ([sleep-edf-expanded](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/sleep-edf-expanded/card.md)), and the candidate strand's is that eight of eleven datasets carry EEG alongside at least one peripheral modality with their status turning on what the source's own analysis did with them ([candidate-datasets-ontology](../research/synthesis/candidate-datasets-ontology.md) §5).

**Theme 6, the comparator and the null.** Six kinds of reference point are used interchangeably and are not exchangeable: another pretrained model and nothing else ([brainwave](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainwave/card.md)), the same architecture without pretraining ([reve-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/reve-2025/card.md)), a small supervised network ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)), classical handcrafted features ([lin-2026-identity-trap](../research/collection/eeg-models/lin-2026-identity-trap/card.md)), a dummy and a chance baseline reported as rows ([neuralbench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/neuralbench/card.md)), and the theoretical chance level, which is shown to be the wrong threshold at small sample sizes ([combrisson-2015-chance-level](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/combrisson-2015-chance-level/card.md)). Exactly one entry in the whole corpus states its own resolution limit and then reports an effect inside it ([hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison/card.md)).

**Theme 7, what the field distributes and on what terms.** Every EEG model paper that releases weights states no licence for them, each card recording the omission individually, and the peripheral strand records the identical position for its two released encoders ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md), [cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md), [papagei-2024](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/papagei-2024/card.md), [science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §10). The one licence stated for any released artefact in the models strand covers benchmark pipeline code rather than weights ([adabrain-bench-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/adabrain-bench-2025/card.md)). On the data side the ladder runs from CC0 through a click-through agreement ([mous-2019](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/mous-2019/card.md)) and a printed, signed, scanned end-user licence ([deap-2012](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/deap-2012/card.md)) to request-to-the-authors with no published route ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

## 3. The argument

### 3.1 A reported transfer number is one position on three axes the corpus has measured to reorder results

The adaptation regime reorders the models, and this is demonstrated inside the corpus rather than asserted between cards. One suite runs both regimes over ten checkpoints and 54 datasets and reports that the model rankings change substantially ([omnieeg-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/omnieeg-bench/card.md)); a second finds the same dependence with the opposite sign for a different checkpoint, EEGPT rising from 25.81 under full fine-tuning to 47.89 under linear probing on BCI-IV-2A while CBraMod falls from 47.71 to 32.32 ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)). Two demonstrations with opposite signs are not two votes; they are a joint demonstration that a result at one regime does not locate a result at another.

The reordering is now also attested first-party and within a single model, which removes the objection that it is an artifact of third-party reimplementation. A partial-fine-tuning table reports five regimes at a fixed holdout on two datasets, and the ordering is not monotone in how much is unfrozen: full fine-tuning gives balanced accuracy 0.8140 on TUAB and 0.6409 on TUEV, unfreezing the last eight of twelve transformer blocks gives 0.8134 and 0.6611, and the linear probe gives 0.7954 and 0.3461 ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)). The best cell in that table is interior. Freezing costs 1.9 points on one dataset and 29.5 on the other, from one checkpoint on its own two headline datasets, which means a project reporting only a frozen number could be understating its checkpoint by thirty points and a project reporting only a fine-tuned number cannot tell which case it is in.

Scale does not order the models, so the checkpoint's identity is a third free axis rather than a proxy for a class. Six independent measurements support the negative ([eeg-models-ontology](../research/synthesis/eeg-models-ontology.md) §2.5), and the two dissents are weaker than they read: one reports improvement with size while fitting no scaling law and showing only a trend ([reve-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/reve-2025/card.md)), and the other cuts against its own side by reporting its tiny model beating its base model on three datasets, 0.886 against 0.877 on MDD, 0.610 against 0.604 on MEG-MMI and 0.863 against 0.832 on SomatoMotor ([brainomni-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainomni-2025/card.md)). The same corpus holds one model frozen beating another fine-tuned, which is the two axes interacting in a single observation ([brainomni-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainomni-2025/card.md)).

One caution belongs here rather than in the limitations, because it bounds this whole subsection. Six checkpoints that benchmark cards record as evaluated have no entry in the models strand, so any sentence of the form "no EEG foundation model does X" carries a bound it must state or close ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §3.3, [adabrain-bench-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/adabrain-bench-2025/card.md)). The claims above are claims about the carded set and are stated as such.

### 3.2 The axes are large relative to the effects, which is what makes a single point uncheckable

The margins the field reports are smaller than the movements the axes produce. In the one suite publishing a full per-dataset table, the margin between the best foundation model and the best supervised model in balanced accuracy runs from minus 0.08 on Sleep-EDF, where CBraMod's 69.47 sits below ST-Transformer's 69.55, to plus 15.00 on EEGMAT, where CBraMod's 88.89 beats Conformer's 73.89, with a median of a few points and no interval reported for any cell ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)). Against a median of a few points, a 46.8-point split effect ([brookshire-2024-data-leakage](../research/collection/eeg-models/brookshire-2024-data-leakage/card.md)) or a 29.5-point regime effect ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)) is not a nuisance parameter but the dominant term.

The interval is wider than the statistic that would naturally be reported, and the corpus holds the bound in a different place from the numbers it should bound. The measurement strand records that the standard error across folds strongly underestimates the true error, worst at a factor of 0.26 for the scheme with the best true error bars, while the model strand's within-paper ablations and the fusion strand's deltas are reported with no bound attached at all ([varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure/card.md), [science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §7). Several fusion entries report an accuracy with no chance level and no class balance, one card observing that "multiclass" without a class count makes 0.8780 uninterpretable as an effect size ([wibirama-cognitive-load-eye-movement](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/wibirama-cognitive-load-eye-movement/card.md)).

Some of the field's numbers also travel rather than being measured, so counting agreements overstates the evidence. One suite takes all baselines from another paper, another quotes numbers displayed in existing studies except for one reproduction, and a fourth takes baseline parameter counts from a fifth; the signature is that disagreements follow the chain rather than being random, with ST-Transformer's parameter count at 3.5M in the two papers descending from one table and 3.2M in the two sharing tables with each other ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md), [reve-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/reve-2025/card.md), [femba-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/femba-2025/card.md), [luna-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/luna-2025/card.md)). One disputed quantity spreads nearly three orders of magnitude and is load-bearing in two strands: BENDR's parameter count is 0.39M, 3.97M, 157M, and unpublished by its own paper ([bendr-2021](../research/collection/eeg-models/bendr-2021/card.md), [zare-2026-stress-testing](../research/collection/eeg-models/zare-2026-stress-testing/card.md)).

Underneath the numbers, the partition that most of them rest on is itself unverified. The naming document for the field's most reported abnormal-EEG split says the data "was divided into two sets" without asserting patient-disjointness, the parent archive averages 1.56 sessions per patient with one contributing 37, and the release note that would settle it sits behind a registration wall; every foundation-model TUAB number in this corpus inherits that partition ([tuab](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/tuab/card.md), [tuh-eeg-corpus](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/tuh-eeg-corpus/card.md)).

### 3.3 The negative literature is the control, and it is populated well enough to be the default expectation

A small supervised or classical model frequently matches or beats a checkpoint, and this is supported by six entries, five of them third-party rather than first-party self-report ([eeg-models-ontology](../research/synthesis/eeg-models-ontology.md) §4.2). EEGNet's 72.29 beats every foundation model on Siena and Conformer's 73.84 does so on HMC ([adabrain-bench-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/adabrain-bench-2025/card.md)); the best fine-tuned foundation model reaches 0.745 against EEGNet's 0.731 with 2,394 trainable parameters ([lee-2025-lbms-capable-yet](../research/collection/eeg-models/lee-2025-lbms-capable-yet/card.md)); classical logistic regression reaches 0.847 against the best foundation-model tier at 0.755 ([lin-2026-identity-trap](../research/collection/eeg-models/lin-2026-identity-trap/card.md)); classical features beat frozen REVE by at least 12.7 points under every tested condition ([zare-2026-stress-testing](../research/collection/eeg-models/zare-2026-stress-testing/card.md)); DeepConvNet is highest at every calibration size ([sirca-2026-peft-motor-imagery](../research/collection/eeg-models/sirca-2026-peft-motor-imagery/card.md)); and the one first-party entry reports its own fine-tuning as "mostly on par with the fully supervised counterpart" ([bendr-2021](../research/collection/eeg-models/bendr-2021/card.md)).

Two qualifications keep that at the right width and both cut against reading it as a verdict on pretraining. One of the six has its magnitude behind a paywall with only its sign on record ([sirca-2026-peft-motor-imagery](../research/collection/eeg-models/sirca-2026-peft-motor-imagery/card.md)), and the corpus's general caution applies to the rest: "there is always a concern that authors applying a method as a baseline may not be using it to its fullest potential", which one card marks as applying to every transfer number in that strand ([guetschel-2024-representation-learning-review](../research/collection/eeg-models/guetschel-2024-representation-learning-review/card.md)). A baseline that is strong because it was tuned and a checkpoint that is weak because it was not are indistinguishable in a table.

The negative literature is also where the axis argument gets its sharpest instance, because the self-supervised advantage has been measured as a curve rather than as a point. It runs at 22.8 points at one label per class, shrinks monotonically, and inverts above roughly 10,000 examples per class, after which full supervision leads by 1.6 to 3.5 percent ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md)). That is the shape of the effect the project is testing, and it is the reason a single operating point is close to uninterpretable near the crossover. One mapping detail restricts what the curve indexes: the contrast is frozen features read out by a linear probe against a fully trained supervised network, which the card records is not the like-for-like fine-tuning comparison that later work in the strand runs, so the curve gives an expectation to the frozen arm and to nothing else ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md)).

### 3.4 The corpus's own falsifications show that the geometry is workable, not merely desirable

The prescription in this paper is not hypothetical, because the corpus contains four instances of a claim being located on an axis and thereby settled or bounded. First, a checkpoint re-pretrained with an evaluation corpus excluded and re-evaluated on it: TUEV 0.6659 plus or minus 0.0124 excluded against 0.6671 plus or minus 0.0107 included, and TUAB 0.8249 plus or minus 0.0025 against 0.8289 plus or minus 0.0022 ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)). Pretraining-corpus overlap is therefore worth 0.12 to 0.40 balanced-accuracy points, inside one standard deviation in both cases. The question was worth asking, since a structured comparison of ten EEG foundation models found that in four of the ten reviewed studies the downstream evaluation datasets were already used for pretraining ([kuruppu-2025-critical-review](../research/collection/eeg-models/kuruppu-2025-critical-review/card.md)); locating the quantity is what turned a suspected confound into a bounded one. That measurement retired a gap and, more usefully for this project, retired a claimed strength: STRUM's absence from every pretraining corpus is worth about a third of a point and should be reported as a property of the design rather than as a contribution.

Second, a suite that publishes both a multi-subject row and a cross-subject row for the same models and tasks, and attributes the difference to the boundary rather than pooling it into a rank: cross-subject performance of three checkpoints on SEED is lower than multi-subject performance by 15, 19 and 7 percent, with the paper concluding that foundation models generalize better across sessions than across subjects ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)). The residue is a reporting-precision complaint rather than an unmeasured quantity: the held-out unit is "sessions or trials" and is not resolved per dataset, so the 15, 19 and 7 cannot be attributed to the session boundary specifically ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)).

Third, the partial-fine-tuning table already cited, which converts "should we freeze or fine-tune" from a methodological preference into a measured two-dataset answer with a non-monotone shape ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)). Fourth, and the only entry in the corpus that meets both halves of the inference standard the measurement strand recommends, a benchmark that reports a dummy and a randomly initialized model as rows and rescales the metric against both ([neuralbench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/neuralbench/card.md), [moabb](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/moabb/card.md)). None of these four is expensive. Each is an extra evaluation or an extra row, not an extra study.

### 3.5 The peripheral question is thinner than its citation count suggests, and supports a direction rather than a relation

The claim that peripheral gains shrink as evaluation tightens is worth stating carefully, because it has taken three discounts and is the load-bearing claim behind the third planned comparison. It was written as resting on three arms with stated splits and it now rests on one measured shrinkage plus one uninformative null. The measured shrinkage is a fusion gain of +5.84 and +6.21 under LOSO against larger figures under 10-fold on the same 21 participants and the same four-modality input ([angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load/card.md)). The null is a difference its authors call not significant under a train-on-early, test-on-late split on 14 participants, and its own recovered Results prose gives the study's resolution limit: "Using the assumption of a binomial distribution, significance (p < 0.05) is reached for differences of around 10%", against a fusion increment of 3 to 5 percent ([hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison/card.md)). That study can exclude a ten-point fusion benefit and cannot exclude a three-point one, so its contribution to a shrinking relation is a null it could not have resolved either way at the sizes under discussion.

The third arm has left the count for a different reason and should not be quietly restored. A near-zero fusion gain of +0.002 sits on an entry whose Methods describe 5-fold subject-independent GroupKFold while its Conclusion states the study used 5-fold cross-validation "without subject-wise separation", so it cannot be counted among the corpus's subject-disjoint evaluations and its gain cannot be read as a subject-independent result ([azad-2025-construction-noise](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/azad-2025-construction-noise/card.md)). A relation between protocol strictness and gain cannot take a data point whose protocol is the thing in dispute. What that entry still supports, independently of its folds, is the separate question of whether the gain is near zero at all, and one further observation that is independent of the split: fusion was applied on the test set only if it beat the EEG-only model on a fusion-validation subset, and a system that gates its own fusion behind a per-fold check is a system whose designers observed that fusion sometimes hurts ([azad-2025-construction-noise](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/azad-2025-construction-noise/card.md)).

Stated at the width the corpus now supports: the direction is the best-supported reading, nothing contradicts it, and it is a suggestive pattern rather than a demonstrated relation. No quantity in this section should be read as an effect-size estimate. Two further data points sit alongside it without changing that status: ECG alone reaches 0.61 against EEG's 0.86 and respiration's 0.70 in the study that stated its own resolution, "just reaching a level that is significantly higher than chance" ([hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison/card.md)); and a recovered per-study row in a review that predates this literature has one study reaching 80 percent on HRV features alone against about 77 percent on HRV plus EDA, a peripheral channel added and the accuracy going down ([haque-hrv-stress-review](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/haque-hrv-stress-review/card.md)).

The two double-digit gains in the corpus are not refuted and are weighted down rather than dismissed, and that weighting is a judgment rather than a finding. One entry describes its split three mutually inconsistent ways ([salam-eeg-ecg-stress](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/salam-eeg-ecg-stress/card.md)); the other attributes its EEG stream to two datasets that distribute no EEG, and since the EEG-only arm is the denominator of the claimed gain, the gain cannot be trusted either ([kumar-2026-attention-eeg-ecg-stress](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/kumar-2026-attention-eeg-ecg-stress/card.md)). A reader who declines the weighting is left with five entries pointing two ways, and that is the honest description of the alternative ([multimodal-biosignals-ontology](../research/synthesis/multimodal-biosignals-ontology.md) §2.1).

One universal that would have made this section tidier is false and is not used. It is not the case that no EEG checkpoint represents a peripheral channel: one is pretrained on resting EEG, sleep EEG and ECG, with an ECG lead owning a row in the same learned channel-name vocabulary as an EEG derivation ([biot-2023](../research/collection/eeg-models/biot-2023/card.md)). What it does not do is take EEG and ECG in one input; it fine-tunes on any of them one modality at a time. The structural bar holds for the two checkpoints it was derived from, which drop auxiliary channels ([bendr-2021](../research/collection/eeg-models/bendr-2021/card.md)) and carry a sensor-type vocabulary with exactly three values ([brainomni-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainomni-2025/card.md)), and it does not generalise past them.

### 3.6 Label validity is three runnable procedures plus one constraint, and the constraint rejects the criterion once attributed to it

The candidate strand's label-validity axis was carded as four tests and is three. Two of the three fix what a spoken-versus-written label can mean rather than checking it: hours of the same narratives listened to and read give semantic tuning that is "highly correlated in most semantically selective regions of cortex", with cross-modality prediction accurate enough that the representations are called "almost identical" ([deniz-2019-modality-invariant-semantics](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/deniz-2019-modality-invariant-semantics/card.md)); and training a decoder in one presentation modality and testing it in another localises the modality-independent category signal, which means whatever distinguishes a spoken trial from a written one is by construction the part that does not generalise ([simanova-2012-modality-independent](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/simanova-2012-modality-independent/card.md)). The third is the removal procedure: post hoc counterbalancing biases accuracy upward, plain confound regression biases it downward far enough to produce significant below-chance performance, and only confound regression performed in every fold of the cross-validation routine restores plausible above-chance performance ([snoek-2019-confound-control](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/snoek-2019-confound-control/card.md)).

The fourth entry is a constraint on interpretation rather than a procedure, and the corpus had its argument backwards until an audit corrected it. Section 4.3 of that paper is titled "Predicting behaviour is not enough" and concludes that "merely predicting behaviour using decodable information is not enough to revive the dictum"; the correct-versus-incorrect-trial study is introduced there as an illustration of the problem, and what the paper proposes instead is to connect behaviour to the structure of the activation space ([ritchie-2019-decoding-limits](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/ritchie-2019-decoding-limits/card.md)). Any sentence recommending a correct/incorrect criterion on that paper's authority would be citing it against itself. The same card records that the paper runs no procedure on any data and that its two accuracy figures are quoted from another study rather than measured, and records a tension it does not resolve: its recommendation to restrict classifier flexibility "sits in direct tension with the entire premise of transfer from a large pretrained model" ([ritchie-2019-decoding-limits](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/ritchie-2019-decoding-limits/card.md)).

Two extensions from a sibling strand measure rather than argue, and they are what make the label question concrete for this project. A planned working-memory study was published as a cautionary result because the memorised item was decodable from horizontal and vertical gaze position, on data already cleaned of blinks and eye movements by a pass the authors call imperfect; the card draws the consequence directly, that reading requires saccades and listening does not, so a spoken-versus-written contrast produces systematic differential ocular behaviour by construction ([mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md)). And four EOG channels alone decode spatial auditory attention above chance when gaze and attention are congruent ([rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias/card.md)). The label axis therefore has the same shape as the protocol axis: the quantity of interest is not the accuracy but the accuracy's position relative to a control that the design either does or does not supply.

### 3.7 STRUM is valuable as a design, not as a sensor inventory

The sensor inventory is genuinely exceptional and is the wrong reason to choose the dataset. STRUM carries 206 EEG channels, 2-channel ECG, 2-channel EOG, a 16-channel respiration belt and a 43-channel EMG neckband, all on the same 24-bit BioSemi amplifier at 512 Hz, which makes it the only recording in the corpus carrying all three of the project's named peripheral modalities and one of four where no cross-device alignment is required at all ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md), [dataset-hierarchy](../research/synthesis/dataset-hierarchy.md) §7). It is also the densest EEG layout in the corpus, 65 percent denser than the densest recording any carded checkpoint has been evaluated on, Chisco at 125 ([brain4fms](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/brain4fms/card.md)). But density compounds badly with a second property: electrode coordinates are absent, the paper not saying whether individual coordinates were digitised per subject or a template is assumed, and a template for a non-standard high-density cap may not exist ([candidate-datasets-ontology](../research/synthesis/candidate-datasets-ontology.md) §4.4). The checkpoints that could ingest 206 channels are precisely the ones that need the field STRUM is missing, leaving one carded checkpoint whose spatial mechanism admits the montage without a name lookup, a coordinate or a selection step, at a 10.8-fold increase over the 19 channels it was pretrained on ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)).

The design properties are the ones with no analogue elsewhere in the corpus. The side tasks form a 2x2 of stimulus modality by stimulus kind, manipulated within subject, and no other recording crosses those two factors; the one recording carrying the same contrast at scale manipulates it between 102 readers and 102 listeners and carries no EEG at all ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md), [mous-2019](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/mous-2019/card.md)). The within-subject label geometry is also the regime where erasing subject identity improves label decoding by 6 to 12 points, because every subject contributes trials in all four cells and identity carries no information about the label, so the identity remedy is both applicable and predicted to help ([lin-2026-identity-trap](../research/collection/eeg-models/lin-2026-identity-trap/card.md)).

The design supplies two levels of identity that the corpus's six-row table has no rows for, and one of them has a published protocol. The session breaks into "5 blocks of 4-6 activities each, with 10-20 second long pauses between blocks", and the dataset's own authors cross-validated on exactly that boundary with nested blockwise CV for hyperparameters, which makes the block the finest holdout unit in this design that is not a segment and the only identity boundary in the project with a published protocol on this exact recording ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md), [science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §2). The second is the co-recorded partner, and its justification is a dependency in the recorded signal rather than in ambient conditions: stimuli are presented over loudspeakers in a room seating both participants and verbal responses are given by voice command, so half of the 2x2's stimulus deliveries and a self-paced fraction of every response are physically shared between two people ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The step from that dependency to "therefore the split unit is the pair-session" is this review's inference and is marked as such; no card treats the co-participant as a level of identity ([gap-analysis](../research/synthesis/gap-analysis.md) §5.3, [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

The consequence for the third planned comparison is where the design reading and the sensor reading come apart most sharply. The two peripheral channels most likely to produce a visible gain would produce it for the wrong reason: EOG through the reading-versus-listening confound, which the 2x2 diagnoses but does not subtract, since gaze differs between reading and listening whether the material is verbal or not ([candidate-datasets-ontology](../research/synthesis/candidate-datasets-ontology.md) §3, [mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md)); and HRV and the respiration belt at a 5-to-20-second integration window against a stimulus-locked label of 2 to 4 seconds they cannot resolve ([wang-2025-sedation-non-eeg](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/wang-2025-sedation-non-eeg/card.md), [mckeen-2025-ecg-fm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mckeen-2025-ecg-fm/card.md)). The remedy is a change of question rather than of method, and it is available on the same recording: the session was designed to induce "a moderate degree of fatigue" over about 3.5 hours, which is a slow within-session covariate at the peripheral window's own time scale, and the dataset's published task-performance analysis is exactly such a label ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

## 4. Anchor case: the dataset paper's own Section V

The existence proof that the reporting geometry is traversable on this substrate is that its authors already traversed part of it, on this recording, with a published protocol. Response-error detection under per-subject blockwise cross-validation gives dual-spectral regularized logistic regression at Az 0.75 plus or minus 0.13, hierarchical discriminant component analysis at 0.71 plus or minus 0.10 and shrinkage linear discriminant analysis at 0.63 plus or minus 0.07, all significantly above the 0.5 chance level, with the differences between the first method and the other two "highly significant at p<0.01" ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). That is a model comparison, matched folds, on the project's own dataset, with the protocol stated. It removes the claim that comparison 1 would establish a first number on STRUM and replaces it with something more useful: an external sanity check on whether a number is reasonable and a protocol to match.

The same section measures the floor, and quoting only the half that helps was one of the errors the Phase 4 revision corrected. Task-performance estimation over 8-second windows gives CSP at 0.55 plus or minus 0.06, spectrally weighted CSP at 0.56 plus or minus 0.07, filter-bank CSP at 0.59 plus or minus 0.07 and cross-validated dual-spectral CSP at 0.61 plus or minus 0.08, all above chance at p<0.01, and then "only the difference between DLCSPcv and CSP was significant at p<0.05" ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). A six-point difference was resolved and a four-point and a two-point difference were not, at a denominator of roughly 50 to 56 subjects, which is more favourable than the pair-session unit this paper recommends. The resolution floor for that family of labels sits between four and six points, and the target effect for comparison 2 has a median of a few points ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)).

Section V also supplies the strongest on-substrate evidence for the ocular argument, and it comes from the people who collected the data rather than from the fusion strand. Of the best response-error detectors: "the highest-performing methods prominently depend on artifactual EEG sources, specifically frontal patterns typical for eye activity for some subjects ... and temporal-lobe patterns typical of muscle activity for some other subjects", generalized in the discussion to "artifacts that may be correlated with user state, and which may be mistaken for brain signatures" ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The contrast the same section draws is what makes it usable rather than merely cautionary: the task-performance models, which are slow and oscillatory rather than stimulus-locked, rest instead on "spatial patterns localizable to neocortical locations", occipital alpha and frontal beta. On this dataset the stimulus-locked label class recruited eye activity and the slow label class did not.

Two things the anchor case does not establish should be stated with it. The published baselines are on different labels, response errors and high-versus-low task performance, so a first result on the modality-by-kind label is still a first on that label; and Section IV's analyses ran on a 64-channel subsample at 128 Hz, so a 206-channel result is not directly comparable without reproducing the subsample ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The subsample is also the precedent for the fallback that would discard the property making a dense evaluation a contribution in the first place ([brain4fms](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/brain4fms/card.md)).

## 5. What the corpus does not support

This section is placed before the roadmap rather than after it, following the gap analysis's own ordering, because the roadmap depends on it ([gap-analysis](../research/synthesis/gap-analysis.md) §5). It covers four kinds of limit: claims blocked by inaccessible entries, questions where two entries point opposite ways, values carded as sourced that are not checkable against the archived source, and places where this paper's own confidence outruns its evidence, the last including the pair-session unit claim that no card supports ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

### 5.1 Claims blocked because the relevant entries are inaccessible

How much of a spoken-versus-written classifier's accuracy is sensory cannot be quantified here. The elimination argument rests on two abstract-only cards: one records that "almost identical" is a qualitative abstract summary and that the residual difference "could be small and real, and this card cannot say how large it is" ([deniz-2019-modality-invariant-semantics](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/deniz-2019-modality-invariant-semantics/card.md)), and the other records that the abstract does not say how large the modality-specific component is relative to the modality-independent one ([simanova-2012-modality-independent](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/simanova-2012-modality-independent/card.md)). The review can say the contrast is not semantic in the way its name suggests; it cannot say by how much.

The empirical chance level for any design in this project is also unavailable. The tabulated levels as a function of sample size, class count, cross-validation parameters and classifier are the part a user would apply, and they are reported but unread ([combrisson-2015-chance-level](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/combrisson-2015-chance-level/card.md)). The corpus supports the claim that the theoretical level is the wrong threshold at small n and supports the remedy, a permutation null; it supports no specific number. Similarly, the implementation of the one confound-removal procedure the corpus recommends is not reconstructible from it, the card stating that no implementation detail should be taken from it, and two further hedges apply: "unbiased" overstates an abstract whose claims are that the negative bias "disappears" and the method "appears to appropriately control for confounds", and the reason per-fold regression works is the card's reading rather than the abstract's ([snoek-2019-confound-control](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/snoek-2019-confound-control/card.md)).

Whether cardiac activity is downstream of the cortical state is a prediction rather than a measurement here. All three convergent-cross-mapping entries are inaccessible on their decisive quantity, and the applied one reports no coefficients, no significance levels and no control for the video stimulus driving both signals, which its card calls the single most important control for a coupling claim of this kind ([zeng-brain-heart-ccm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/zeng-brain-heart-ccm/card.md), [schiecke-2019-brain-heart-ccm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/schiecke-2019-brain-heart-ccm/card.md)). Its detected direction may also be biased by a single-lead ECG reconstructing a cardiac attractor more cleanly than 32-channel EEG reconstructs a cortical one. Separately, how many fusion studies declined to run the physiology-only arm cannot be counted: four entries computed the comparison and the number did not reach the corpus, and the rule that those four must not be counted as evidence about the literature is stated explicitly and is honoured here ([multimodal-biosignals-ontology](../research/synthesis/multimodal-biosignals-ontology.md) §1.4, [scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §3.1).

### 5.2 Questions where two entries point opposite ways with no basis to choose

Whether ocular activity is artifact or signal is not a live disagreement that weighting will settle, because the instrument differs across the fault line. Every entry arguing that ocular activity is signal built its decoder from an eye tracker ([wibirama-cognitive-load-eye-movement](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/wibirama-cognitive-load-eye-movement/card.md), [ahmad-2020-cognitive-load-framework](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/ahmad-2020-cognitive-load-framework/card.md), [hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison/card.md)), and the one entry that builds a decoder from EOG electrodes is on the other side ([rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias/card.md)). The one recording carrying both instruments on the same trials at 1200 Hz builds a decoder from only the tracker ([mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md)). Any reasoning of the form "eye tracking carries cognitive load, therefore EOG will help" crosses a boundary the corpus does not license.

Three further questions are unresolved in the same way and are named rather than adjudicated. Which adaptation regime is primary has five suite positions with no entry converting between them, and two suites demonstrating the reordering with opposite signs ([datasets-benchmarks-ontology](../research/synthesis/datasets-benchmarks-ontology.md) §2.2, [omnieeg-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/omnieeg-bench/card.md)). Whether scale orders the models has six measured observations saying no and two saying partly yes, and one of the six rests on a parameter count held at three orders of magnitude of spread ([bendr-2021](../research/collection/eeg-models/bendr-2021/card.md), [zare-2026-stress-testing](../research/collection/eeg-models/zare-2026-stress-testing/card.md)). And whether peripheral physiology adds information beyond EEG has three entries consistent with near-zero and two inconsistent, with the weighting applied in section 3.5 marked as a judgment ([multimodal-biosignals-ontology](../research/synthesis/multimodal-biosignals-ontology.md) §2.1).

### 5.3 Values marked unverified, which must not be laundered into this paper as established

Several numbers in the corpus are carded as sourced and turn out not to be checkable against the archived source, and the mark does not travel with the number unless a document carries it ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §5). Four instances are on record: a confidence-bound table at n = 30, 300 and 1000 whose body is not in the committed extraction and of whose four rows only n = 100 is stated in prose ([varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure/card.md)); thirteen accuracy values where re-extraction does not help because the table bodies are not in the PDF's text layer ([eegconformer-2023](../research/collection/eeg-models/eegconformer-2023/card.md)); a set of routinely-excluded anomalous subjects that no source read here states ([physionet-mi](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/physionet-mi/card.md)); and a preprocessed sampling rate ([deap-2012](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/deap-2012/card.md)). This paper quotes the n = 30 row of the verbatim Appendix A.2 table rather than the unverified Table 1, and states no confidence bound derived from the latter.

A second class is values whose supporting tables are absent from the source rather than from the extraction. Linear-probing figures for the applicable peripheral encoder are in supplementary tables that are not part of the archived source, so what the corpus has is the qualitative shape only: at the smallest training-set sizes the frozen probe "outperforms the baselines and performs comparably to Full", and then "its performance plateaus because it lacks the representational capacity necessary to exploit additional downstream data" ([mckeen-2025-ecg-fm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mckeen-2025-ecg-fm/card.md)). That shape is used in this paper; no number is attributed to it.

### 5.4 Where this paper's own confidence outruns the corpus

Three claims here are this review's inference rather than a card's, and are marked at the point of use as well as here. The pair-session as the unit of the split is an inference from a quoted dependency, not a card's claim, and the corpus's identity table has no row for the co-recorded partner ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md), [science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §2). The labelled-trial count that positions STRUM on the label-count curve is arithmetic on a sentence describing "ca. 200 non-repeating statement sentences" as the shared stimulus set of two verbal tasks, giving roughly 5,000 to 5,600 pooled examples per class on the straightforward reading and about 11,000 on the doubled one, with a plausible floor well below that if distractor presentations do not count as labelled examples ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). And the cross-participant audio contamination of the visual cells is this review's reading of a paragraph about loudspeakers and independent pseudo-random schedules; no source in the corpus raises it ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

Two claims the project might expect to make about STRUM's factorial rest on one sentence of one paper, read once through institutional access, with no second reader and no released data to check against ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The 2x2 is a design property as quoted, not a validated one: whether its cells are balanced and whether they are matched on stimulus duration is unrecorded, the two visual cells sit on different screens, the two auditory cells differ in spatialisation, and which side screen each auditory cell occupies is not stated by the paper at all. The paper also contradicts itself on channel count, 206 in section III against 205 in section IV, and on participants, 56 as 28 pairs against a sex breakdown summing to 62; a reconciliation exists, that "datasets" in "3 datasets were excluded" means pair-sessions, and it is an arithmetic coincidence offered as a hypothesis rather than a resolution ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

Finally, an absence that would have been convenient is a resource limit rather than a property of the field, and bounds every "no checkpoint does X" sentence in this paper. Six checkpoints named by benchmark cards as evaluated have no entry in the models strand, and two follow-on consequences are already on record: one model's parameter count is held at two values three orders of magnitude apart with no primary in the corpus to adjudicate, and another is counted among the represented model families while the strand holds no entry for it ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §3.3, [luna-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/luna-2025/card.md)).

## 6. How much the citation guarantee is worth

Every claim in this paper links to a card, and that guarantee is worth stating precisely rather than assuming. A corpus-wide audit checked all 84 cards against their own sources and found 57 carrying at least one defect; the gap analysis this paper is built on cites 47 of those 57 across 149 links ([gap-analysis](../research/synthesis/gap-analysis.md) §8, [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). No gap was retracted as a result and seven still survive, but several arguments narrowed, and the narrowings are the reason this paper states three of its central claims smaller than an earlier draft of the synthesis layer did.

The defects fall into three classes with different implications, and only the third is invisible to careful reading. The first class is unchecked falsifiers: four gaps were retracted in an adversarial pass because the falsifier each gap itself named was already satisfied by a card in the corpus, in three cases by a card the gap cited ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md), [cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md), [papagei-2024](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/papagei-2024/card.md), [brainomni-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainomni-2025/card.md)). The second class is arguments carried one step past their source: a design fact generalised beyond the sentence stating it, a curve read outside the arm it measures, a significance result quoted for the comparisons it resolved and not the ones it did not ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md), [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

The third class is the one that matters for a reader relying on the links: claims that were true of the cards and false of the sources. One card had its paper's argument backwards, enumerating as an available remedy the option its source explicitly rejects ([ritchie-2019-decoding-limits](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/ritchie-2019-decoding-limits/card.md)).

**One quotation was fabricated**, and it is the worst single defect the audit found. A card attributed to its source a sentence about generative adversarial networks that does not occur there, alongside a proportion the source never states; the source's actual claim points the same way, which is presumably how the invention survived collection, an ontology, a cross-strand map and three reviews ([guetschel-2024-representation-learning-review](../research/collection/eeg-models/guetschel-2024-representation-learning-review/card.md)). This paper cites that card elsewhere. A quotation reads as the most verified object on a card, which is exactly why nobody rechecked it, and an earlier draft of this section named a different and more forgiving case in its place: an electrooculography prohibition that does not exist in its card's source because the string belongs to a different data set in the same review and survived only through an appended document that a re-extraction dropped ([bci-competition-iv-2a](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/bci-competition-iv-2a/card.md)). That second case is an accident of tooling. The first is an invention, and a section pricing this review's reliability that reported the accident and omitted the invention was mispricing it. A second unsourceable claim held that a BIDS sidecar records a machine-readable channel type, which neither BIDS card's source supports ([eeg-bids](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/eeg-bids/card.md), [eeg-bids-2019](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/eeg-bids-2019/card.md)). One card denied a fact its own source stated, that a recording carries both EOG electrodes and an eye tracker on the same trials ([mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md)). And one card attributed a re-pretraining number to the wrong row, which propagated as a claim that the delta was not on record ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)).

**The largest class of defect was neither of the above, and it was self-inflicted.** Most findings in one strand, and many elsewhere, were cards asserting in the present tense that a table "did not survive" extraction or "collapsed into unusable fragments". That was true of the markitdown output those cards were written against; every source was later regenerated with a better converter, the regeneration was recorded in each entry's metadata, and the card bodies describing the old failure were left standing. The result was a population of false absence claims, which is the single most damaging input to a gap analysis, since a gap is an argument from absence. Recovering them returned real evidence in both directions: a review's per-study table in which adding electrodermal activity to heart-rate variability lowers accuracy ([haque-hrv-stress-review](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/haque-hrv-stress-review/card.md)), and a workload study's per-sensor arms including electrocardiography alone, together with its own statement that significance there requires about ten points where its fusion increment was three to five ([hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison/card.md)).

Two cards remain partly unverifiable and no audit can fix them, because the evidence is absent from the archived source rather than misread: thirteen accuracies whose table bodies are in neither the extraction nor the source PDF's text layer, of which one dataset's three figures have no corroborating prose at all ([eegconformer-2023](../research/collection/eeg-models/eegconformer-2023/card.md)), and a confidence-bound table whose body did not survive extraction, so this paper uses the appendix bounds that did ([varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure/card.md)).

The corrections did not all run one way, and saying so is part of the accounting. Two cards gained evidence: a checkpoint paper turned out to test frozen representations after all, in an appendix table nobody had read, which converted a reporting-frequency argument into a first-party measurement ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)); and a benchmark suite turned out to name all eighteen of its evaluation datasets in a table the earlier card had transcribed only half of, none of the eighteen being a language or stimulus-modality task, which halved a bound in the gap it constrained ([brain4fms](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/brain4fms/card.md)). Two others lost evidence in ways that weakened conclusions this paper would have preferred: a cross-modal transfer precedent turned out to be means over four hand-picked high-performing subjects rather than a twenty-subject group result ([simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories/card.md)), and a fusion null turned out to be underpowered against the effect it was cited for ([hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison/card.md)).

Two facts about the audit itself bound what it establishes. It was executed by four agents, one per strand, and its corrections were propagated into the synthesis layer by four more, with the propagation verified by re-checking each corrected entry's uses rather than by rereading the documents; so the same class of error that the audit found is possible in the audit's own output, and the sampling that would detect it has not been run. And a first exhaustive pass finding 57 defects in 84 cards bounds nothing about what a second pass would find. The rate is a measurement of what was there, not a certificate of what remains.

What the guarantee therefore buys is traceability, not correctness, and the distinction is operational. A cite-card link lets a reader land on the card and check the claim against `source.md`, which is what caught all of the above; it does not certify that the card was right when written, and the third defect class is precisely the failure a citation discipline cannot catch by being applied more carefully, only by auditing the layer beneath it ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §6). One structural defect in the review's own method is also on record and is inherited here: the scope check licenses "property of the field" on the disjunct that a card states an absence about itself, which is sound for a review that surveyed the field and unsound for a primary study reporting what it did not run. Seven of the original eleven gaps cleared on that disjunct and two of those seven were wrong; three survivors still rest only on it. They are marked where the argument sections use them and, as an adversarial pass found, were not marked in the roadmap; section 7 now marks them at the point of use ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §6, [rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/rotaru-2024-auditory-attention-bias/card.md)).

## 7. Roadmap

The roadmap has one precondition and it is not a research act. STRUM sits at the highest-friction rung of the access ladder, request to the authors with no published route, `oa_status: closed`, no hosted copy found by any registry sweep, and a data licence that is unknown rather than restrictive ([candidate-datasets-ontology](../research/synthesis/candidate-datasets-ontology.md) §7.1, [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). Five of the seven actionable routes depend on that request succeeding, and the request should carry the coordinate or cap-layout question, the EOG placement question, the auditory-task screen assignment and the usable session count with it, as one action with a go/no-go outcome.

**Phase A, before any data arrives.** Two checks cost a line each and both are blockers. The field's reference reader is recorded as reading BTI/4D, KIT, EDF, BioSemi BDF, BrainVision and FIF natively and explicitly not GDF, with XDF on neither list, while STRUM is XDF written by the LSL toolchain ([mne-python](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/mne-python/card.md), [lsl-2024](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/lsl-2024/card.md)). The second is the project's own memory constraint: the lazy-loading sentence the reader's card quotes has the FIF format as its subject rather than the reader class, and the paper does not extend the claim to the EDF, BDF or BrainVision readers a STRUM pipeline would hit ([mne-python](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/mne-python/card.md)). Phase A also settles the licence precondition for any checkpoint the project would fine-tune and redistribute, which no source in the corpus states ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §10, [labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)).

**Phase B, on open substrates, independent of the request.** Two of the seven routes have open fallbacks and should run first as rehearsals. Route A3 closes gap L2 by running closed-form LEACE erasure of the subject axis and cross-validated confound regression on the same embeddings and the same folds and reporting both, which is the comparison no entry runs; two precedents exist for the shape of the experiment and neither crosses this pair ([lin-2026-identity-trap](../research/collection/eeg-models/lin-2026-identity-trap/card.md), [snoek-2019-confound-control](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/snoek-2019-confound-control/card.md)). Its open substrate is CC BY 4.0 and downloadable, with lead-I ECG and horizontal EOG on the same amplifier, subject to two hazards its card names: participant identifiers are not stable across repeats, so a naive subject-wise split would leak, and transcranial electrical stimulation is applied in every session, which the card calls the reason it is not a clean substrate for representation learning, with neither the raw nor the display-filtered view clean at the ramp boundaries. Since route A3 runs erasure and confound regression on embeddings, that second hazard bears on exactly this use and the rehearsal should be read as a mechanics check rather than as an estimate ([tes-eeg-ecg-2021](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/tes-eeg-ecg-2021/card.md)). Route A6 is the cheapest of the seven: the twelve recurring HRV indices computed through a standard library against a pretrained ECG encoder's embeddings, one task, one split, with the EEG arm alongside, and it keeps its experiment while losing its novelty claim because the comparison exists in two other forms already ([haque-hrv-stress-review](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/haque-hrv-stress-review/card.md), [makowski-2021-neurokit2](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/makowski-2021-neurokit2/card.md), [angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load/card.md)). Route A7's open rehearsal is a deposited recording carrying both ocular instruments at 1200 Hz with data and scripts on record, which never built a decoder from the EOG side ([mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md)).

**Phase C, comparison 1 as a label-count curve.** The commitment is to report the from-scratch baseline at several label counts rather than at one operating point, following the design of the curve the corpus already measured ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md)). The rationale is neither that a null would be uninformative nor that it would be especially damning; near the crossover the measured advantage in either direction is small enough that a single point says least, and STRUM's estimated label count lands in that flat region ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The artifact is a curve with the published protocol matched to the dataset paper's blockwise CV, comparable to its Section V numbers on the labels they share ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

**Phase D, comparison 2 as three regimes and two checkpoints.** Report the frozen probe, a partial fine-tune and a full fine-tune, on evidence rather than on a reporting-frequency count: freezing costs 1.9 points on one dataset and 29.5 on another, and unfreezing the last eight of twelve blocks is the best cell in that table ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)). Run at least two checkpoints with different spatial mechanisms so that a null is attributable to transfer rather than to one channel contract, which given the coordinate absence means the context-generated one plus a name-indexed one under stated channel selection ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md), [eegpt-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/eegpt-2024/card.md)). Route A2 rides on this phase and is narrower than it once looked: dense downstream evaluation is not unprecedented across modalities, so the claim available is a scalp-EEG checkpoint above roughly 137 channels ([brainomni-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainomni-2025/card.md), [adabrain-bench-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/adabrain-bench-2025/card.md)). The fallback if the montage cannot be fine-tuned at 206 channels is not only to freeze, since partial fine-tuning is a third position ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)).

**Phase E, the split geometry, which applies to every phase above.** Report the pair-session-disjoint arm and the subject-disjoint arm and state the difference, following the dataset authors' blockwise protocol with nested blockwise CV for hyperparameters ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). Fit and evaluate within pair-session and test the distribution of per-pair scores across pairs, which is the recommendation the measurement strand makes for this design class, and run a permutation null because it is cheap and correct rather than because n is fatal ([varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure/card.md), [combrisson-2015-chance-level](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/combrisson-2015-chance-level/card.md)). Decide the generalisation claim in advance: the narrower within-population protocol is permitted where the inference is narrower, provided the paper says so ([kamrud-2021-data-partitioning](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/kamrud-2021-data-partitioning/card.md)).

**Phase F, comparison 3 run as a falsification rather than as a measurement of added value.** Four commitments, each tied to a gap. Audit the cross-participant audio contamination of the visual-verbal cell first, since both participants' event timelines are in one XDF file so the co-occurrence rate is computable exactly and contaminated trials can be excluded or modelled ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). Report the physiology-only arm, since only four entries in the whole corpus run three arms on one split and a physiology-only floor costs one extra model ([multimodal-biosignals-ontology](../research/synthesis/multimodal-biosignals-ontology.md) §1.1). Run A5 and A7 as one campaign on the same instrumentation and the same request, testing the ocular gain against the non-verbal cells and building the ocular arm from sign-invariant features, vertical deflection, blink rate and saccade rate as a magnitude, which is the family the dataset's own authors observed contaminating their models and the family for which the transfer diagnostic returns the correct answer ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md), [mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md)). And do not make the stimulus-locked label the primary target for HRV or respiration; run those channels against the slow fatigue covariate the session was designed to induce, reporting the stimulus-locked arm as a resolution-limited secondary ([wang-2025-sedation-non-eeg](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/wang-2025-sedation-non-eeg/card.md), [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

**Phase G, the label question, which an adversarial pass found this roadmap had failed to schedule at all.** It is the project's own label, the gap is a survivor, and the route is available on no other dataset in the corpus, so its absence from an earlier version of this section was an omission rather than a decision. The design is the one the dataset's factorial structure supplies: train the spoken-versus-written contrast on the verbal cells and test on the non-verbal cells, where a classifier that transfers is reading stimulus modality rather than language ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md), [candidate-datasets-ontology](../research/synthesis/candidate-datasets-ontology.md) §3). Two constraints attach. The transfer precedent this design would otherwise lean on is means over four hand-picked high-performing subjects rather than a group result, so it sets no expectation and must not be quoted as one ([simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/simanova-2010-eeg-object-categories/card.md)). And the two visual cells sit on different screens, so a signed horizontal ocular feature carries opposite signs across them and is unusable as a transfer feature, which is why Phase F builds the ocular arm from sign-invariant features instead. This gap rests on the self-declaration disjunct discussed in section 6 and is marked accordingly.

**Route A4, two frozen encoders, is scheduled last and is the most contingent.** It closes gap L6 by bolting a head onto a frozen EEG checkpoint and a frozen peripheral checkpoint and reporting the EEG-only arm on the same split ([papagei-2024](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/papagei-2024/card.md), [mckeen-2025-ecg-fm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mckeen-2025-ecg-fm/card.md)). Only the ECG encoder is applicable, since the other is PPG and STRUM has no PPG, and its 5-second segments at 500 Hz are long relative to a stimulus-locked epoch and short relative to reliable HRV estimation ([mckeen-2025-ecg-fm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mckeen-2025-ecg-fm/card.md)). The expectation is a frozen peripheral encoder that is competitive in the small-data regime and then plateaus, which is the regime STRUM is in and which caps what the route can show ([mckeen-2025-ecg-fm](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mckeen-2025-ecg-fm/card.md)).

One retracted gap leaves a task that is actionable but not on this substrate, and it is recorded so the roadmap is not read as covering it. Disambiguating a session boundary from a trial boundary needs a multi-session recording, and STRUM is single-session by design, the whole experiment running about 3.5 hours partly to induce fatigue ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)); the substrate would be a 29-participant recording with three sessions one week apart, open on Zenodo, in BIDS, and the corpus's only recording with measured electrode coordinates from a per-session head-and-cap scan, subject to two hazards on its card, that Cz is absent for participants 1 through 9 and that 29 participants is a small pool for any subject-general transfer claim ([hinss-2023-passive-bci](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/hinss-2023-passive-bci/card.md)).

Two surviving gaps are not actionable here and are recorded rather than promised. A measured cross-participant synchronization precision matters for cross-brain analysis, which is outside the review's partition, and STRUM's two participants sit in one time-synchronized file with peripherals on the shared amplifier, so no cross-participant alignment is required by any of the three comparisons ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md), [lsl-2024](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/lsl-2024/card.md)). And the concentration of open dyadic releases in one laboratory is not closable by a research act: STRUM is the dense, other-group release the gap describes as missing, and what is missing is its distribution ([boa-actors-2025](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/boa-actors-2025/card.md), [livewire-2024](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/livewire-2024/card.md)).

## 8. Coordination with adjacent efforts

The largest adjacent effort is one this review deliberately does not touch, and the boundary should be respected rather than blurred. Hyperscanning connectivity measures, inter-brain coupling and their critiques went out of scope when the team-neuroergonomics strand was replaced, the exclusion was encountered in practice and honoured, and the works it turned away are named on record ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §1.1, [candidate-datasets-ontology](../research/synthesis/candidate-datasets-ontology.md) §6.1). The brief's remedy is a partition change, a new strand rather than a widened one, and nothing in this paper proposes it. Multi-person recordings themselves remained in scope and are carded ([boa-actors-2025](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/boa-actors-2025/card.md), [livewire-2024](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/livewire-2024/card.md), [amigos-2021](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/amigos-2021/card.md)).

The benchmark suites are the natural venue for the axis-reporting argument and the natural adopters of it, since two of them already publish the raw material. One publishes both regimes over ten checkpoints and 54 datasets ([omnieeg-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/omnieeg-bench/card.md)) and one publishes both a multi-subject and a cross-subject setting on the same models and tasks with an MIT-licensed pipeline ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md), [adabrain-bench-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/adabrain-bench-2025/card.md)). What neither crosses is regime with holdout unit, which is the single cell the corpus most obviously lacks and which a suite could fill more cheaply than a single-dataset project ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §1). A third suite implements the inference standard the measurement strand recommends and would be the natural place to add the interval ([neuralbench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/neuralbench/card.md), [moabb](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/moabb/card.md)).

Two coordination items are legal and archival rather than scientific. No released checkpoint of either modality in this corpus carries a licence, so the terms under which the project may fine-tune and redistribute any of them are unstated by every source, and that is a precondition to settle with the checkpoint authors rather than a direction to pursue ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §10, [cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)). And the annotation standard the project's own dataset uses, HED tags under an ontology extended from HED 1.0, is the one an archive-side entry argues for and identifies as precisely the gap in which a label like spoken versus written gets defined inconsistently across datasets, which makes the released event stream valuable to more than this project ([nemar-2022](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/nemar-2022/card.md), [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

## 9. Discussion

### 9.1 Interpretation

If the argument is adopted, the unit of publication in this literature changes from a number to a located number, and several existing disputes dissolve into coordinate differences. The dispute about whether foundation models beat supervised baselines becomes a question about where on the label-count axis and under which regime, with a published curve already available for one arm ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md)) and a published non-monotone regime table for another ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)). The dispute about whether peripheral physiology helps becomes a question about the protocol strictness and the temporal scale at which the question was asked, which is what separates a measured six-point shrinkage from an underpowered null ([angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/angkan-2024-invehicle-cognitive-load/card.md), [hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/hogervorst-2014-workload-comparison/card.md)).

For this project specifically, the interpretation reorders what counts as the contribution. Being unseen by every pretraining corpus is worth about a third of a balanced-accuracy point and is a design property to state in one sentence ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)); being unlike everything the checkpoints saw, at 206 channels in a dyadic room with a task battery, is the large and unmeasured term and is the risk rather than the asset ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The contribution that survives is the located result: a transfer number reported at three regimes, two holdout units and two checkpoints, on a design where the label varies within subject and the identity remedy is predicted to help rather than hurt ([lin-2026-identity-trap](../research/collection/eeg-models/lin-2026-identity-trap/card.md)).

### 9.2 Limitations, and the corpus entries that do not support this thesis

Several entries cut against the thesis and are named rather than omitted. The clearest is the one clean positive in the corpus's most adversarial entry: cross-subject ictal detection at 0.793 AUROC against 0.701 for a randomly initialised encoder, 0.505 for random features and 0.500 under label permutation, in a paper whose entire design is a negative-control battery ([zare-2026-stress-testing](../research/collection/eeg-models/zare-2026-stress-testing/card.md)). Transfer is not uniformly unlocatable; when the design puts positives and negatives inside the same recording sessions, the result is clean and the axes stop mattering. A second is the self-supervised advantage at the small-label end, 22.8 points at one label per class, which is a large effect that no axis argument diminishes ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md)). A third is the plus 15.00 margin on EEGMAT, which is far outside any interval the corpus would put on it ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)).

On the peripheral side, two entries report double-digit fusion gains and have not been shown wrong ([salam-eeg-ecg-stress](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/salam-eeg-ecg-stress/card.md), [kumar-2026-attention-eeg-ecg-stress](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/kumar-2026-attention-eeg-ecg-stress/card.md)); their denominators have been shown unverifiable, which is a different thing. And cross-modality pretraining is not a dead end: one checkpoint pretrains jointly on EEG and MEG with an ablation and reports downstream numbers at 269, 306 and 372 sensors, which is a working demonstration that a single model can carry more than one sensor type ([brainomni-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/brainomni-2025/card.md)).

The corpus's own limits bound the thesis as well as its evidence. This is not a systematic review: there is no PRISMA accounting, no protocol registration, no screening log and no inter-rater agreement on inclusion, every acceptance criterion is a floor, and discovery ran outward from a user-supplied seed set along the citation graph ([scope-diagram](../research/synthesis/scope-diagram.md) §5). Counting cards is not counting studies, and four distinct mechanisms by which the same measurement appears several times are on record ([science-map](../research/synthesis/science-map.md) §11, [labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)). A head-to-head confound-control comparison published in the neuroimaging methods literature and cited by no EEG paper in this corpus would not have been reached ([snoek-2019-confound-control](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/snoek-2019-confound-control/card.md)).

### 9.3 The strongest objection, and why the answer this paper first gave to it fails

The strongest objection is that the prescription is unaffordable on the substrate it is prescribed for, and the corpus supplies the arithmetic. The dataset's own authors resolved model differences at a denominator of roughly 50 to 56 subjects and failed to resolve others of the same size ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The effect this project must detect has a median of a few points across a full per-dataset margin table ([adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)). And the pair-session unit this paper recommends reduces the denominator further.

An earlier version of this section answered the objection on three grounds. An adversarial pass refuted all three, and the refutations are recorded here rather than removed, because the objection is the load-bearing one and a reader is entitled to see what did not survive.

The first answer held that an unresolved difference at a stated position still bounds something. Its exemplar was a re-pretraining delta of +0.0012, which is a *resolved* near-zero with an interval an order of magnitude narrower than the effect it excludes ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)); it is evidence for the value of narrow intervals, not for the value of unresolved positions. An unresolved null at this substrate's denominator carries an interval wider than the entire target-effect range, so it excludes nothing within it ([varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/varoquaux-2018-cross-validation-failure/card.md)).

The second answer held that the cheap axis positions carry most of the argument. They do not. Subject-disjoint and pair-session-disjoint splits differ in their *training* sets, so the second holdout arm is a second fit rather than a second evaluation of a fitted model. The roadmap's phases compose multiplicatively rather than additively, and the fatigue covariate the third comparison is redirected onto is not an annotation this dataset carries; the source says only that the session length was chosen to induce fatigue, so the label would have to be constructed and validated before it could be used ([strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)).

The third answer held that the objection's premise argues for the axis, since the floor is known only because the dataset paper reported several differences rather than one. This equivocates. All of that paper's comparisons sit at one position on all three axes; it varied *method*, which is not an axis, and the floor is known because it reported dispersion and significance tests. That is an argument for reporting intervals, not for traversing positions.

**What follows from conceding.** Backing an effect-size estimate out of the dataset paper's own reported dispersion puts the minimum detectable difference near seven points at the full subject denominator and near ten at the pair-session denominator, against a target distribution whose median is under three points and of which roughly one cell in thirteen exceeds ten. Correcting for the number of cells a full crossing implies pushes the detectable difference higher still. The honest conclusion is that a full crossing of three axes on this substrate multiplies underpowered points, and that this paper's prescription, stated as a crossing, was wrong.

The thesis survives the correction; the prescription changes shape. What makes a transfer number falsifiable is that its position is stated and its interval is reported, not that many positions are visited. So the recommendation is to state the coordinates, report an interval and a permutation null at every point, and traverse only the cheapest axis, which is the adaptation regime, since three regimes are three fits on one training set and the corpus contains a first-party measurement of what that axis alone is worth ([labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md)). The holdout axis should be crossed once, as a stated conservative arm, not factorially. Any pre-registration should carry an explicit minimum detectable difference and should prune arms that cannot reach it, which the roadmap in section 7 now reflects.

A second and weaker objection, that the benchmark suites already do this, does not hold: neither crosses regime with holdout unit, five suite positions exist with no entry converting between them, and the two demonstrations disagree in sign, which is what makes an unlocated single-checkpoint result uninterpretable rather than merely imprecise ([datasets-benchmarks-ontology](../research/synthesis/datasets-benchmarks-ontology.md) §2.2, [neuralbench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/neuralbench/card.md)).
---

## 10. Conclusion

The corpus supports a narrower and more useful conclusion than either "foundation models transfer" or "foundation models do not transfer". It supports the claim that most published transfer numbers cannot be checked against each other, because the holdout unit, the adaptation regime and the checkpoint identity move results by more than the results differ, and because the same corpus that measured those movements almost never reports its own numbers as positions on them ([brookshire-2024-data-leakage](../research/collection/eeg-models/brookshire-2024-data-leakage/card.md), [labram-2024](../research/collection/eeg-models/labram-2024/card.md), [adabrain-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/adabrain-bench/card.md)). Where a claim has been located, it has been settled or bounded cheaply, which is the evidence that the prescription is workable rather than merely tidy ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md)).

For this project, the three planned comparisons come out sound, conditional and not-as-posed in that order. Comparison 1 is sound and improves by becoming a curve ([banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg](../research/collection/eeg-models/banville-2021-self-supervised-eeg/card.md)). Comparison 2 is sound in premise and conditional in execution, with one claimed strength withdrawn to about a third of a point and a second narrowed to the frozen arm; stated as "we fine-tuned checkpoint X and it beat a from-scratch baseline" it would claim considerably more than it establishes ([cbramod-2025](../research/collection/eeg-models/cbramod-2025/card.md), [omnieeg-bench](../research/collection/datasets-benchmarks/omnieeg-bench/card.md)). Comparison 3, as posed, would measure a confound on the ocular side and an unresolvable window on the cardiac and respiratory side, and becomes a contribution only when run as a falsification and pointed at a label that lives at the peripheral time scale ([mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds](../research/collection/multimodal-biosignals/mostert-2018-eye-movement-confounds/card.md), [strum-2018](../research/collection/candidate-datasets/strum-2018/card.md)). The project's peripheral inventory is genuinely exceptional; the originally planned label is the wrong thing to spend it on.
